# Canonical render signature

Use the approved EzBuilder workshop scene as the visual-quality reference for
all anhpt.dev raster artwork. Reuse its rendering language, not its room layout,
mountain window, furniture, or publishing props.

## Shared visual traits

- Polished, original 16-bit-inspired pixel art with visibly crisp square pixel
  clusters and stepped dark contours.
- No blur, antialiasing, gradients, painterly strokes, vector-smooth curves, or
  photorealistic texture.
- Restrained deep-emerald and forest-green foundation, warm cream highlights,
  sparing gold focus accents, and muted red identity accents.
- Warm directional light with one coherent source. Use compact highlight and
  shadow clusters to model wood, stone, cloth, metal, paper, and foliage.
- Clear foreground, middle ground, and background separation. Detailed focal
  areas are balanced by calm negative space reserved for semantic HTML.
- Dense craft detail is welcome only when silhouettes and reading order remain
  clear at the consuming component's actual display size.

## Prompt kernel

Include this concise kernel in generation prompts, adapted to the asset:

> Original polished 16-bit-inspired pixel art; crisp square pixel clusters;
> stepped dark outlines; restrained deep emerald, forest green, warm cream,
> sparing gold, and muted red palette; one warm directional light source; clear
> depth and readable silhouettes; detailed natural materials; no gradients,
> blur, antialiasing, painterly texture, photorealism, readable text, logos,
> copied interfaces, or watermark.

Do not mention a copyrighted game, character, named location, or screenshot as
a style reference.
